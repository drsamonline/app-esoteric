Esoteric —

A symbol in two traditions is two entries, not one.

There is no field called meaning in this dictionary. There is a reading, and a reading belongs to a tradition, and every tradition is named with its source. The same lotus is not one thing read two ways; it is three entries that happen to share a word.

Search a symbol and you get the traditions that used it, side by side, each attributed — because the moment a dictionary says what a symbol “really” means, it has stopped describing and started preaching.
